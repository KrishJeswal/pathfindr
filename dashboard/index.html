Smart Indoor Navigation & Faculty Monitoring System

An integrated, web-based smart-campus solution that helps students, faculty, and visitors move through complex academic buildings with ease. It combines SVG-based interactive indoor navigation with a real-time, RFID-driven faculty availability tracker — reducing wasted time, cutting dependency on manual directions, and improving coordination across campus.
